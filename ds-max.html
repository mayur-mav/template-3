BUILDING THE JOY OF LIVING.

Founded on January 18, 2007, by Dr. K. V. Satish, DS-MAX Properties Pvt. Ltd. has established nearly two decades of excellence in Bengaluru's real estate market. Guided by its core motto, "Joy of Living," the developer focuses on delivering quality, luxurious residential communities at affordable prices while maintaining complete RERA compliance and customer satisfaction.

With a solid track record of completing 160 projects, DS-MAX Properties has brought modern homes to over 22,000 happy families. The builder continues its strong growth with more than 30 ongoing projects in key locations across Bengaluru, combining strategic connectivity with state-of-the-art security and lifestyle amenities.
